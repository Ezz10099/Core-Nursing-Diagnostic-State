## Tracking rules
- Correct + confident + correct reasoning: strong/provisional mastery; reduce immediate retesting.
- Correct + guessed/weak reasoning/missing concept: hidden gap; remediate and retest.
- Wrong + unsure: clear knowledge gap; remediate and retest.
- Wrong + confident: misconception; highest-priority remediation and retest.
- Mastered material returns only as occasional spaced retention checks.
